## Surf level and progression?

This retreat is ideal for L2 lower intermediate to L5 advanced surfers. Below we have outlined the recommended minimum and maximum level to get the most out of this retreat.

**Minimum level:** You can pass through whitewater, broken waves and catching waves independently. You are riding down the line consistently and looking to improve basic surf technique.

**Maximum level:** You want to progress with barrel riding technique and aerial manoeuvres.

Throughout the week, you’ll work closely with Christian to pinpoint the habits or techniques limiting your performance and refine them to help you break plateaus in your surfing.

With Damara Surf’s unique surf progression program, our personalised and professional approach promises to unlock years of potential and take your skills to the next level.

## HOW TO GET HERE

Your airport transfer is included in your package! Be sure to fly into Velana International Airport (MLE) in Male. We will transfer you to Thulusdhoo Island by a 30 minute boat ride and taxi from boat to our accommodation.

## Yoga

The yoga classes are designed to fit surfers, with or without a yoga experience. Through dynamic and creative vinyasa flow we will target different key movement, to improve flexibilty and balance. With gentle hatha we will focus on stretching muscle group put to a high stress while surfing. Both type of classes will include a meditation/breathing exercise.  
Mobility class will provide an upper and lower body mobility routine to help you reach or keep a range of movement essential for your surf practice.

---

## Surf guiding and surf lessons – what’s the difference?

Surf lessons are longer structured surf sessions (usually taking place in the morning) – expect personalised in-water coaching + 1 coach filming all your waves from land/boat.

The guided sessions (usually afternoons) are optional free surfs. The boat transfers for these guided sessions are not included in the retreat package (transfers range from €8-€20). The surf coaches will surf on every guided session, advising on surf spot and helping with positioning but expect a more friendly in-water vibe than coaching.

These guided sessions also give you some flexibility to do other activities like snorkelling trips, or simply relax and unwind.

## Is this a surf camp or a surf retreat?

Our Maldives trip is a unique blend of both. While we call it a surf coaching retreat because of the personalised coaching and premium accommodation, it also offers the laid-back, social vibe you’d expect from a classic surf camp. You’ll enjoy daily surf sessions, video analysis, island adventures, and the chance to connect with a like-minded surf community — all in a beautiful, tropical setting.
